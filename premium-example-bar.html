Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do you take reservations?

The bar and most tables are walk-in, first come first served. For groups, the lounge is bookable, call or message us and we'll set it aside.

Is there a happy hour?

Yes, Tuesday through Friday, 4 to 6pm, with a rotating list of cocktails and bar bites at a discount.

Is the patio heated?

Yes, the street-side patio is heated and lit, so it's comfortable most evenings year-round.

Do you serve food?

A full bar-bites menu, fries, charcuterie, oysters, sliders and snacks, served until close.

Can you host private events?

Yes. The lounge holds groups up to 30, and we can buy out the room for larger parties. Reach out and we'll plan it with you.

Is there a dress code?

None. Come as you are, whether that's straight from the office or out for the night.
