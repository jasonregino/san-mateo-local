Shoreview

Shoreview is San Mateo's northeast, bay-side neighborhood, a residential area with its own local restaurants, markets, and services.

San Mateo Local lists 28 local, independent businesses in Shoreview, San Mateo, from restaurants and shops to everyday services. Nobody pays to be listed.

Where to Eat & Drink

Deli / sandwiches · $

An old-school Shoreview deli known for classic build-your-own sandwiches, roast beef, pastrami, and turkey, piled with all the fixings.

Aldo's Pizza

Pizza · $$

San Mateo's oldest pizzeria, serving Shoreview since 1958, known for a thin, crispy pie and old-school specialties. Nearly closed this summer; the neighborhood is keeping it open, so go grab a pie while you still can.

Things to Do

Shoreview Park

park · Free

An east-side neighborhood park with a skate park, playground, and picnic facilities.

Bayside/Joinville Park

Bayfront park

A large community park at the mouth of Marina Lagoon with ball fields, tennis courts, a soccer field, an off-leash dog park, a playground, picnic areas, and the seasonal Joinville Swim Center.

Harborview Park

Neighborhood park

A neighborhood park with a baseball field, half basketball court, playgrounds for younger and older kids, and picnic areas.

Parkside Aquatic Park

Aquatic park

A lagoon-side park with a sandy beach, a roped-off swim area, a boat launch, a playground, and seasonal concessions along Marina Lagoon (no lifeguard on duty).

Bayside Dog Park

Dog park

A friendly, fenced off-leash dog park inside Bayside/Joinville Park, smaller and more laid-back than the big Seal Point park, and a neighborhood favorite where the regulars all know each other.
