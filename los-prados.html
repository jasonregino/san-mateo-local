Los Prados

Los Prados sits on San Mateo's bay side near Marina Lagoon, a residential area with neighborhood restaurants and services close to the water.

San Mateo Local lists 23 local, independent businesses in Los Prados, San Mateo, from restaurants and shops to everyday services. Nobody pays to be listed.
