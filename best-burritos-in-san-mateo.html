The Best Burritos in San Mateo, According to Locals

Updated August 16, 2026

We asked San Mateo where it actually gets its burritos. Over a hundred locals answered on r/SanMateo. Here is where the recommendations kept landing.

We didn't want to build this one off Google stars. San Mateo has too many longtime counters, food trucks, and hole-in-the-wall gems for a star rating to mean much. So we asked the people who actually eat here.

We put it to r/SanMateo: your top three burrito spots, and the one that flies under the radar. One rule, no chains, local only. Over a hundred of you answered, way more than we expected, with longtime favorites, very specific orders, and some strong opinions.

Here is the honest part. A few of the most-loved spots are places I have not even been to yet. El Palenque blew up the whole thread, practically in my backyard, and somehow I have never made it in. That is exactly what we hoped would happen. San Mateo Local was never about me telling you what is best. It is about finding out what San Mateo actually loves.

So this is not a scientific ranking. It is where the recommendations kept landing, with my own notes where I have a dog in the fight.

The Best Burritos in San Mateo is built from what over a hundred locals said on r/SanMateo, plus notes from Jason where he has actually eaten. Nobody paid to be on it.

The names that kept coming up

El Palenque Taqueria

The thread's standout · North Shoreview

Its recommendation was the most upvoted answer in the whole thread, by a wide margin. Honest note from me: I have not actually made it over to North Shoreview for this one yet, but when this many locals point to the same place, you listen.

Charlie's Taqueria

My recent go-to · Downtown, B St

This is where I keep landing lately. A lot of burrito for the money, and that stretch of B St has less traffic and easier parking.

La Cumbre Taqueria

Mission-style classic · Downtown, B St

A downtown mainstay by the Transit Center, from the lineage credited with the original Mission-style burrito. Old-school, hefty, and the kind of place people stay loyal to for years. Regulars order it super, no rice, half carne and half al pastor.

Las Palomas Taqueria

Downtown, 3rd Ave

Big, loyal following. A lot of people in the thread miss the old location near Jack in the Box and admit they have not been back since it moved. I have had the new spot for takeout and it still holds up. Plenty of folks swear by it.

Taquería Los Moreno

The hidden gem · 43rd Ave

The thread's favorite sleeper. I tried it a few years back and the burrito was the size of my forearm. Not everyone agrees, but the people who love it really love it.

Taqueria Pancho Villa

The old institution · Downtown, 4th Ave

A place people have gone to since they were kids. Also the most honest debate in the thread: plenty say it was the best in town years ago and liked it better back then. The fish taco still gets defended.

Trucks and stands worth chasing

Leo's Tacos Truck

El Camino Real

Great al pastor. Regulars order it super, no rice.

La Carreta

Food truck · near Bridgepointe

Parked on the corner near Bridgepointe, and you will almost always see a line. Several people said it beats a lot of the sit-down spots, which is high praise for a truck.

Jayli's Cocina

Food truck · 28th Ave

The wet mole burrito is the one to get.

El Sabor de Mi Pueblo

Truck · N Humboldt

Parks by the military surplus, weekdays only. A quiet neighborhood favorite.

Kelly's Deli

Stand at Home Depot · Mariners Island

The breakfast-burrito pick. A stand parked at the Home Depot on Chess Drive, turning out big breakfast burritos for shoppers and local workers.

Value picks and sleepers

Suavecito Birria & Tacos

Amphlett

The move here is the 24K California burrito with tots. That is the one people rave about. Fair warning from the thread: opinions split on the regular burrito, but the cali burrito has real fans.

Guadalajara Grill

Laurie Meadows

I forget about it because it is not close to me, but it is genuinely good. Big grilled-chicken supers, and free soda refills.

Taqueria Los Primos

Downtown, N B St

Something about their carne hits different. It was cash-only the last time I went, so worth checking before you go.

S El Camino Taqueria

25th Avenue

The steak and prawn super burrito combo has a loyal following. Solid al pastor too.

Ay Caray Taqueria

Shoreview

Right by the Chavez market. The rice gets specific shout-outs, which tells you they sweat the details.
