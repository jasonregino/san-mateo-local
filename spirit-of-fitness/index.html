About

Hello, I'm Rhonda

My primary objective as a fitness professional is to motivate and educate my clients, teaching safe and effective exercises in order to achieve their fitness goals. I work with my clients to help them determine their own objectives for total health and well-being, and I develop tailored plans by setting out step-by-step achievable goals.

What are your interests? What makes you move? Start by doing activities you enjoy, and I will be your guide. Whether you prefer training one on one, are looking for a group exercise class that suits your needs, or would like to train with a friend, I can help.

I teach in San Mateo, California, and online. Everything I make is designed to be done anywhere, with very little equipment, at whatever level you are starting from.
