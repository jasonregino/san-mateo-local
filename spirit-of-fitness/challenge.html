What is in it

Thirty days, built to be finished

Twenty-two workouts, four easy days, and four full rest days. Most workouts are between ten and twenty minutes. The longest is on the final day, and that is on purpose.

Everything is standing or low impact. No jumping. Almost no floor work. Where equipment is used it is light dumbbells, a resistance band, or a weighted bar, and a broomstick will stand in for the bar if you do not have one.

Day 1 and Day 29 are deliberately the same workout. Do it at the start, then do it again four weeks later, and notice what changed.

Before you start

A note on safety

This program is designed to be gentle, but you know your body and your history better than I do. Check with your doctor before beginning any new exercise program, especially if you are managing a condition, recovering from an injury or surgery, or have not exercised in a while.

Move at your own pace. Every workout can be made easier, and I show modifications as I go. If something hurts, stop.
