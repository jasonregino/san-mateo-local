Good to Know

Frequently asked

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes. New clients are welcome, and your first visit includes a free 15-minute consult so we can point you to the right care. Book online or give us a call.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most PPO plans for chiropractic and physical therapy and file the claim for you. Acupuncture and massage are often covered too, send us your plan and we'll check before your visit.

What should I expect on the first visit?

A real assessment, not a rushed adjustment. We talk through your history and goals, then build a plan that fits, and we tell you honestly how many visits it should take.

Can I get an appointment this week?

Usually, yes. We keep same-week slots open and have evening hours Monday through Friday.
