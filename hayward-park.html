Hayward Park

Hayward Park runs along the Caltrain line in the middle of town, a walkable pocket with its own small cluster of restaurants and neighborhood businesses.

San Mateo Local lists 39 hand-picked local businesses in Hayward Park, San Mateo, from restaurants and shops to everyday services, with no paid placement.

Where to Eat & Drink

Bucky's Cocktail Lounge

Neighborhood lounge · $$

A renovated neighborhood lounge with dark oak counters, a deep beer-and-whiskey lineup, a back patio, and yard games like cornhole and darts.

Steamies Bar

Dive bar · $

A San Mateo watering hole for more than fifty years and a longtime old-timers' hangout. After sitting closed for over a year, the owners secured a long-term lease, gutted and remodeled the space, and reopened the doors in March 2008. It's a laid-back dive with a pool table, game-day TVs, a patio, midweek karaoke, and taco trucks parked outside.

The Pantry

American / Filipino · $

A longtime family-run kitchen serving hearty American breakfast and lunch alongside Filipino classics, a Hayward Park favorite for decades.

Suavecito Birria & Tacos
