Hayward Park

Hayward Park runs along the Caltrain line in the middle of town, a walkable pocket with its own small cluster of restaurants and neighborhood businesses.

San Mateo Local lists 38 local, independent businesses in Hayward Park, San Mateo, from restaurants and shops to everyday services. Nobody pays to be listed.
