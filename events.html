SEP26
Cars

San Mateo End of Summer Car Cruise

6:00 to 10:00 PM · El Camino Real, between Hillsdale Mall and 3rd Ave, San Mateo · Bay Area Cruise Night

Classic and custom cars cruise a two-mile stretch of El Camino Real, turning around at Hillsdale Mall on one end and 3rd Ave on the other. It is a cruise rather than a car show, so the cars stay moving. Free to watch from the sidewalk.

September Arts & Culture Fest

A free day-long downtown celebration of art, music, dance, poetry, and food, with live mural painting and local artists.

OCT1
Workshop

Secrets of Stunning Photographs

11:00 AM to 12:00 PM · Main Library (Oak Room), 55 W Third Ave, San Mateo · San Mateo Public Library

Photographer Mary Bartnikowski shows how to take remarkable photos with just your phone: seeing light, composing a shot, choosing your angle, and timing. She shares images from her solo travels across 34 countries, plus a quick exercise on getting a great portrait in under a minute. Bring your phone. Free, and no registration required.

OCT6
Workshop

Climate Adaptation Plan Workshop

6:00 to 7:00 PM · Main Library (Oak Room), 55 W Third Ave, San Mateo · City of San Mateo

A community workshop on how San Mateo prepares for sea level rise, and a chance to give input on the plan that protects local infrastructure and natural resources. Refreshments provided.

Workshop

AI Is Here to Empower You: Small Business Survival Guide

5:30 to 7:00 PM · Main Library (Oak Room), 55 W Third Ave, San Mateo · San Mateo Public Library

A free, interactive workshop for small business owners on putting AI to work without the stress. Featured speaker Michael Pessavento of PezTek Consulting, with co-hosts Ed Diaz and Adam Chinn. Practical tips for today's digital landscape, plus light bites and non-alcoholic drinks.

Community

East Hillsdale Park Reopening

1:00 PM · East Hillsdale Park, 123 31st Ave, San Mateo · City of San Mateo

A short ceremony celebrating the reopening of East Hillsdale Park after months of construction and improvements. The park sits in a residential neighborhood with street parking only, so plan accordingly and avoid blocking driveways.

Community

Spooky Book Faire

11:00 AM to 4:00 PM · Main Library (Oak Room), 55 W Third Ave, San Mateo · San Mateo Public Library

An adults' Halloween event presented by HorrorAddicts.net Press, with live scary story readings, table games, door prizes, refreshments, and a photo booth. Books by featured authors available for purchase. Free admission.

Workshop

Ready, Set, Own! Buying Your First Home

6:30 to 7:30 PM · Main Library (Oak Room), 55 W Third Ave, San Mateo · San Mateo Public Library

Local real estate professional Betcy Jacob walks through planning a first home purchase: the credit score you need, first-time buyer and down payment assistance programs, and more. Registration is required.

HEAD WEST Marketplace

11:00 AM to 5:00 PM · B Street between 1st and 3rd Aves, Downtown San Mateo · HEAD WEST

Downtown's outdoor makers market takes over B Street on the third Saturday of the month. Browse local makers, merchants, crafters, designers, and artists, from hand-made ceramics to vintage, with downtown's shops and restaurants right there.

Community

Cal Water Centennial Celebration

11:00 AM to 3:00 PM · College of San Mateo, Plaza of the Sun Amphitheater, 1700 W Hillsdale Blvd · California Water Service

California Water Service marks 100 years with an afternoon of live music, food, family activities and giveaways. You can meet the local Cal Water team, talk with community organizations, and take home a free drought-tolerant plant.
