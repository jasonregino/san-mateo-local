Weekend Jazz, the Saturday Market, and a Sunday Adoption Pop-Up

Updated August 18, 2026

Your guide to this week around town: live jazz on B Street Friday and Saturday, the Saturday farmers' market, a Color Me Mine grand opening and a pet adoption pop-up on Sunday, plus trivia and karaoke most nights.

A relaxed late-summer week downtown. Two nights of jazz on B Street, the dependable Saturday market, and a Sunday worth leaving the house for, with a grand opening and an adoption pop-up back to back.

Out this weekend

Weekend Jazz Nights at B Street & Vine

Friday & Saturday · 6–9pm · Downtown

Live jazz two nights running at B Street & Vine. An easy, low-key downtown evening out.

Saturday Farmers' Market

Saturday · 9am–1pm · College of San Mateo

The reliable Saturday market at College of San Mateo: California produce, flowers, baked goods, and hot food, rain or shine.

Color Me Mine Grand Opening

Sunday · 10:30am–12:30pm · Downtown

The paint-your-own-pottery studio marks its grand opening downtown. A good weekend activity for families, no weather required.

Paw-fect Match Adoption Pop-Up

Sunday · 1–3pm · B Street (1st–2nd Ave)

A pet adoption pop-up right on B Street between 1st and 2nd. Stop by to meet some adoptable animals, even if you are only there to say hello.

Friday Night Out

Friday · Downtown

Two ways to spend Friday night: a Latin Night dance party at Motion Arts Center, or live jazz at A2 Wine Bar.

Trivia & Karaoke All Week

Tuesday–Sunday · Downtown

Trivia at McGovern's and O'Neill's midweek, plus karaoke at Blue Moon and McGovern's most nights. The usual downtown rhythm.
