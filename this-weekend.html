Free Back-to-School Haircuts, Jazz Nights, and the Saturday Market

Updated August 7, 2026

Your guide to this week around town: free back-to-school haircuts downtown Sunday, weekend jazz on B Street, the big Saturday farmers market, plus trivia and karaoke most nights and a packed few weekends coming up.

A quieter weekend downtown before a busy stretch. Free haircuts for the kids Sunday, jazz on B Street, a yoga immersion, and the dependable Saturday market, with HEAD WEST, Central Bark, and more right around the corner. Here is what is worth leaving the house for.

A guide to 14 free back-to-school haircuts, jazz nights, and the saturday market spots in San Mateo, put together by a local who eats here. Nobody pays to be on it.

Out this weekend

Free Back-to-School Haircuts at Full Circle Barbershop

Sunday, Aug 9 · 9am–1pm · Downtown

Full Circle is giving free back-to-school haircuts. A genuinely nice one for local families getting ready for the new year.

Weekend Jazz Nights at B Street & Vine

Friday & Saturday · 6pm–9pm · 320 S B St

The family-run wine and bruschetta spot brings live jazz to the weekend, paired with a real happy hour and Italian cafe plates.

Blissology Superflow Immersion at Nandi Yoga

Friday–Sunday, Aug 7–9 · 309 8th Ave

A multi-day flow-yoga immersion at the downtown studio. A good one if you have been meaning to deepen your practice.

San Mateo Farmers’ Market

Saturday · 9am–1pm · College of San Mateo

The biggest market around, 80-plus vendors with produce, flowers, food trucks, and prepared food. The reliable weekend staple.

Trivia & karaoke downtown

Trivia Night at McGovern’s

Tuesday, Aug 11 · 8pm–10pm

Grab a team and settle in for pub trivia at the downtown Irish bar.

Sunset Trivia at O’Neill’s

Wednesday, Aug 12 · 7:15pm

Midweek trivia with a pint at the corner pub on B Street.

Karaoke at Blue Moon Bar

Wednesday, Aug 12 · 9:30pm · 215 S B St

The reliable midweek karaoke standby. Sign up and take the mic.

Karaoke at McGovern’s

Thursday, Aug 13 · 9:30pm

Round two of karaoke for the week, this time at McGovern’s.

Cheap fun

Cinemark Summer Movie Clubhouse

All week · $1.75 tickets

Family movies for a buck seventy-five at Cinemark. A cheap, easy way to beat the heat with the kids.

Coming up

HEAD WEST Marketplace on B Street

Saturday, Aug 15 · 11am–5pm · B Street

The makers market returns to B Street with local makers, crafters, artists, and food. Third Saturday of every month.

Central Bark

Sunday, Aug 16 · 12pm–3pm · Fitzgerald Field, Central Park

A free family and dog celebration with dog contests, demos, pet-friendly vendors, and community resources. Presented by San Mateo PAL and Parks & Rec.

Paw-fect Match Adoption Pop-Up

Sunday, Aug 23 · 1pm–3pm · B Street at 1st Ave

The DSMA and Peninsula Humane Society & SPCA bring adoptable pets to B Street. Start an application on-site.

Main Library 20th Anniversary + PLUR Coffee Grand Opening

Saturday, Aug 29 · 11am–3pm

The Main Library turns 20 with music, dance, and cultural performances, plus the grand opening of PLUR Coffee’s new third-floor cafe.

September Nights on B Street

Sept 10 & 17 · 6pm–8pm · B Street

Live music, food, and a kids zone return to B Street: Motown and R&B from Pure Ecstasy on the 10th, Top 40 with Neon Velvet on the 17th.
