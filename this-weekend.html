The Ice Rink Is Back, Latin Night, and a Late-Night Mixtape This Weekend

Updated November 19, 2026

San Mateo on Ice opens in Central Park. Plus salsa and bachata at Motion Arts, a tango party, Midnight Mixtape at O'Neill's, a Turkish cooking workshop, and Friendsgiving at Color Me Mine.

The big news this weekend: San Mateo on Ice is open in Central Park, through January 10. Friday is Latin Night at Motion Arts Center, Saturday brings a tango party, a Turkish cooking workshop and Midnight Mixtape at O'Neill's, and Sunday is Friendsgiving painting at Color Me Mine. One change: the HEAD WEST Marketplace moved to December 12 because of the weather. Here is what is worth your time.

Out this weekend

San Mateo on Ice is open

Daily through Jan 10 · Central Park, 50 E 5th Ave

The outdoor holiday rink is back, with 11,000 square feet of real ice. Public skate sessions start at $24.61, ages 4 and up. Book online.

Latin Night: Salsa & Bachata

Friday, Nov 20 · from 7pm · Motion Arts Center, 217 S Claremont St

An all-levels salsa and bachata basics class at 7, beginner bachata at 8, then social dancing with DJ Jesica from 9.

Henderson Legacy jewelry pop-up

Through Saturday, Nov 21 · 10am to 5:30pm · 100 S Ellsworth Ave, Suite 607

The last days of a five-day pop-up at Christensen & Rafferty, featuring Florence hand-enameled jewelry.

College of San Mateo Farmers' Market

Saturday, Nov 21 · 9am to 1pm · College of San Mateo

The big year-round Saturday market up on West Hillsdale. Go early for parking and the best pick.

Irish Names: tracing your Irish roots

Saturday, Nov 21 · 10:30am to noon · Online via Zoom

Sean Conley of the San Mateo County Genealogical Society on Irish surnames and getting past brick walls in your family tree. Register with the library for the Zoom link.

Turkish Stuffed Grape Leaves Workshop

Saturday, Nov 21 · 6 to 9pm · Anatolian Cafe & Market, 637 S B St

A hands-on workshop with the cafe's chef, with Turkish tea, sweets and your own grape leaves to take home. Spots are limited, call 650-445-7273.

Milonga Sentimental: Tango Party

Saturday, Nov 21 · 8pm to midnight · Motion Arts Center

A night of tango music and dancing with DJ Gustavo Hornos. $20 at the door or sign up with Motion Arts.

Midnight Mixtape at O'Neill's

Saturday, Nov 21 · 9pm to midnight · O'Neill's Irish Pub, 34 S B St

A late-night music and dance party downtown. 21 and up, no cover.

Chocolate Tempering & Tasting Class

Sunday, Nov 22 · 10:30am to 12:30pm · Baking Arts, 18 E 3rd Ave

Learn to temper chocolate with instructor Rosaura Rivera, taste a range of chocolates, and make treats to take home.

Friendsgiving at Color Me Mine

Sunday, Nov 22 · 10am to 8pm · Color Me Mine, 43 E 3rd Ave

Bring your crew, snacks and drinks and paint the day away. Groups of four or more get $10 studio fees, no reservation needed.

Trivia, Karaoke, and Bar Bingo

All week · Downtown

Bar Bingo at Blue Moon on Tuesdays, trivia at McGovern’s on Tuesdays and O’Neill’s on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and karaoke at Blue Moon, McGovern’s, and O’Neill’s most nights.

Heads-ups

HEAD WEST Marketplace moved to Dec 12

Was Saturday, Nov 21 · B Street

Because of the weather, this month's makers market on B Street is rescheduled to Saturday, December 12.

Los Prados construction starts late November

S Norfolk St at La Selva St and Los Prados St

The city is rebuilding the Los Prados sewer lift station starting late November, through fall 2027. The turn lane from South Norfolk onto La Selva and part of the sidewalk will close, with a temporary walkway in place.

Get ready for storm season

City of San Mateo

The city has posted tips for getting ready for storm season and El Niño.

Coming up

Small Business Saturday downtown

Saturday, Nov 28

Holiday music on B Street from Aragon High's jazz ensemble The Birds (1 to 3pm), a vintage designer fashion pop-up at Golden Moon Studios, and $5 Grinch tickets at Cinemark.

Matcha Strawberry Roll Cake Class

Sunday, Nov 29 · 10:30am · Baking Arts

Make a matcha chiffon sponge rolled with strawberry whipped cream, and take the whole cake home.

Holiday crafting nights

Friday, Dec 4 · Color Me Mine and Doho

An ornament painting party with a hot cocoa bar at Color Me Mine, or a crepe paper poinsettia workshop at Doho.

HEAD WEST Marketplace

Saturday, Dec 12 · B Street

The rescheduled makers market. A good start on holiday gifts.
