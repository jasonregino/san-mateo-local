Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Always. Walk in any time we're open and we'll get you in the next open chair. If you want a specific barber or time, booking online guarantees it.

How do I book a specific barber?

Book online and pick your barber and time, about thirty seconds. Or call the shop and we'll set it up.

Yes, twelve and under is a kids cut. We're patient with first haircuts.
