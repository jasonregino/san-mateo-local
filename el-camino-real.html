El Camino Real

El Camino Real is the four-mile spine of San Mateo, running the whole length of the city. It is not a neighborhood on its own; the businesses along it sit in Downtown, Baywood, the 25th Avenue area, and Hillsdale. This page pulls them all together in one place, block by block down the strip.

San Mateo Local lists 98 local, independent businesses in El Camino Real, San Mateo, from restaurants and shops to everyday services. Nobody pays to be listed.
