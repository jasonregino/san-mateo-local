About Simple Delights

Simple Delights took over J J's Ice Cream and Frozen Yogurt at the Laurelwood Shopping Center in San Mateo in October 2019 when the owner decided to retire. J J's had been in business for over 30 years, and we like to continue the tradition of a small, local business.

Our goal is to provide simple but quality treats, and to serve everyone with a smile and respect. We specialize in custom cakes and desserts for all celebrations, as well as serving ice cream and frozen yogurt. Come visit us for a simply delightful experience at Simple Delights!
