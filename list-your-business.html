Three different moments

Search, research, discovery.

Think about the different moments when someone finds a local business. They're not the same, and a business that shows up in all three is in a far stronger position than one that only shows up in one.

Search

Google

Where people go when they already know what they want. They type "plumber San Mateo," they get results. Pure intent, and Google is great at it.

Research

Yelp

Where people compare. They've got a few candidates and now they're reading reviews, deciding who to trust.

Discovery

San Mateo Local

The moment before all that. "I need someone good, someone local, someone I can actually trust." When a recommendation matters more than a search result.

Search and AI discovery

A straight word about SEO, AEO, and GEO

You've been hearing these three. Here's what they actually mean for a local business, without the jargon and without the overpromising.

🔍

SEO

When we publish a page mentioning your business and linking to your site, that's another locally relevant reference on the web. We can't promise it moves your rankings, and we won't. But it's generally a useful thing to have.

🗣️

AEO

People increasingly ask questions instead of keywords ("who are the best plumbers in San Mateo?"). We structure our content so answer engines can understand what a business does and where. Clear, organized information beats none.

🤖

GEO

When someone asks ChatGPT or Gemini a local question, those tools pull from structured content across the web. We build location-specific content around real local businesses. No guarantee any AI cites you, but this is the kind of information they draw from.

The honest version: the more useful, accurate, locally specific information exists about your business online, the better positioned you are for how people increasingly find things. San Mateo Local is one more way to build that.
