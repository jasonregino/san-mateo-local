Built to be found

Your local presence can help beyond San Mateo Local

There's another reason we build our pages the way we do. Search is changing.

People aren't only typing keywords into Google anymore. They're asking Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI-powered tools questions in everyday language. That's why we build San Mateo Local with SEO, AEO, and GEO in mind.

🔍

SEO

Search Engine Optimization. When your business is mentioned on relevant pages, those pages add locally relevant information about you and a link back to your website.

🗣️

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization. People increasingly ask questions instead of keywords. Our content is structured to clearly describe local businesses, services, and categories so answer engines can understand it.

🤖

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization. By creating structured, location-specific content around real San Mateo businesses, we build pages designed to be understood by AI systems, not just traditional search.

We can't promise that Google or an AI platform will rank or recommend any particular business. What we can do is build the kind of useful, structured, locally relevant information that gives your business another opportunity to be discovered.

Real discovery
