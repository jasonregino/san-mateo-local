Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do you take reservations?

Walk-ins only for parties under six. Larger groups can call ahead and we'll do our best to seat you together.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Most of the breakfast menu can be made vegetarian, and we always have a vegan plate and oat-milk drinks.

Is there parking?

Street parking on B Street and the city garage one block over on 2nd Avenue.

Are you dog-friendly?

Dogs are welcome on the back patio.
