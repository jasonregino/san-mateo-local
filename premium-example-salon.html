Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do I need a consultation before color?

For a big change or a correction, yes, and it's free. For a root touch-up or trim, just book and come in. When in doubt, book the consultation and we'll plan it together.

How do I book a specific stylist?

Book online and pick your stylist and time, about thirty seconds. Or call the studio and we'll set it up.

Do you do color corrections?

Yes. Color correction is by consultation so we can quote it honestly and plan the right number of sessions.

Is there parking?

Street parking on B Street and the city garage a block over on 2nd.
