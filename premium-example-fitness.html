Good to Know

Frequently asked

Is my first class really free?

Yes. Book any class on the schedule as your free intro, no card and no catch. It is the best way to feel the room before you decide.

I'm a total beginner. Will I keep up?

Absolutely. Every class is scaled, and the coach shows you easier and harder options for each move. Beginners are exactly who we build for.

What should I bring?

Water, comfortable clothes, and training shoes. We have mats, equipment, showers, and lockers here.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

Yes. Everything is month-to-month with no join fee. Freeze it while you travel or cancel anytime, just give us a heads up.
