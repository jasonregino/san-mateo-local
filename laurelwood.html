Laurelwood

Laurelwood is a southwest San Mateo neighborhood anchored by its namesake shopping center, with the everyday restaurants, services, and shops locals rely on.

San Mateo Local lists 29 local, independent businesses in Laurelwood, San Mateo, from restaurants and shops to everyday services. Nobody pays to be listed.

Things to Do

Laurelwood Park & Sugarloaf

trail · Free

A creekside neighborhood park whose dirt trails climb Sugarloaf Mountain for ridge-top views over the Peninsula.

The Marvegos Fine Art School

classes · $$

A fine-art school teaching kids and teens drawing, painting, sculpting, and mixed media through structured projects.

Gymboree Play & Music

classes · $$

Parent-and-child play, music, and art classes for babies through preschoolers, plus open gym and party space.

Casanova Park

Neighborhood park

A neighborhood park with a fenced playground, basketball court, open lawn, and picnic areas, linked to Laurie Meadows Park by a bridge over Laurel Creek.

Laurie Meadows Park

Neighborhood park

A 7.5-acre park with a big playground featuring tube slides and a rock-climbing wall, a toddler playground, a fenced dog park, grassy fields, and picnic areas.

Laurie Meadows Dog Park

Dog park

A recently renovated, fully fenced off-leash dog area inside Laurie Meadows Park, which connects to Casanova Park by a footbridge over Laurel Creek.
