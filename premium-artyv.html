Meet Chloe

ARTYV is led by Chloe Sohyun Jo, a STOTT Pilates certified instructor and YogaWorks RYT200 with a pre- and postnatal specialty. Before San Mateo, she ran her own private studio in Korea for more than four years. She opened ARTYV in 2026 and teaches every session herself, one-on-one or duet, never a group class.

Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do you offer group classes or just private sessions?

ARTYV is private by design. Sessions are one-on-one or duet, so you get focused attention every time.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Private sessions are built around your level, whether it is your first time on a reformer or you have practiced for years.

Do you offer pre- and postnatal Pilates?

Yes. ARTYV offers pre- and postnatal training from a certified specialist.

How do I book?

By appointment. Call (650) 654-8550 or message ARTYV on Instagram at @artyv_studio.
