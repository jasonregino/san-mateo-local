★ Local's pick Diner

Heidi's Pies

El Camino Real · $$

A San Mateo classic since 1970, famous for dozens of house-made-from-scratch pies served alongside all-day American diner fare.

Why it's a Local's pick: I have been coming here since I was a kid. Baseball at Trinta Park, then my parents would bring me here after, and I would get a burger while they picked up dinner to take home. The pies are still the reason: banana cream, chocolate cream, and the sour cream and blueberry, which sounds wrong and is not. They almost closed in 2025 over the rent and got three more years, so go while you can.
