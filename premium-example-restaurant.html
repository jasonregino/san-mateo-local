Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do you take reservations?

Yes, and we recommend them, especially Thursday through Saturday. The bar and a few counter seats are kept for walk-ins.

Is the patio heated?

Yes, the street-side patio is heated and covered, so it's comfortable most evenings year-round. Ask for it when you book.

Can you handle dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. There's always a vegetarian option, and a vegetarian tasting menu is available on request. Tell us about allergies when you book.

Do you do private events?

Yes. We have a semi-private space for groups up to 20 and can buy out the room for larger parties. Call or email and we'll plan it with you.

Is there parking?

Street parking downtown plus the city garage on 2nd Avenue, a short walk away.
