# San Mateo Local → Bay Crest Media: Customer Value Ladder

*Last updated: 2026-07-31 · Approved in foreman (ChatGPT) review, 9.8/10*

## The principle

We don't sell a website. We don't even sell "AI." We solve **one problem at a time.** Each yes earns the next conversation. The customer never feels sold.

## The ladder

| # | Rung | The ask | Price | Problem it solves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free Listing** | None. "You're featured on San Mateo Local." | $0 | Gets them discovered. Pure value first. |
| 2 | **Verified Listing** | "Confirm your info, send your website." | $0 | Accuracy + the "raise your hand" moment. Now we're in a real conversation. |
| 3 | **AI Receptionist** | "Can we make sure you never miss a call?" | **Starting at $299/mo** + one-time setup | Missed calls = lost jobs. The core recurring product. |
| 4 | **Website** | "Want a proper site so people can find and trust you?" | Build + monthly | Many trades have no real site. |
| 5 | **Reviews** | "Want us to keep your Google reviews strong?" | Monthly | Reactivate past customers, respond, stay ranked. |
| 6 | **Full Growth System** | "Want the whole thing working together?" | ~$500/mo | Receptionist + reviews + lead follow-up + rebooking. The end state. |

## The flywheel (why this is a machine, not a service)

```
Traffic → San Mateo Local → Claim Listing → Warm Relationship
   → AI Receptionist → Website → Reviews → Recurring Revenue
```

Every piece feeds the next. Far harder for a competitor to copy than "I sell AI receptionists."

## Pricing note (locked)

AI Receptionist is **"starting at $299/mo"** + one-time setup. Some businesses need extra call minutes, some won't. "Starting at" gives room without discounting or overthinking. Do not discount the $299.

## Rules

- One problem at a time. Never pitch rung 6 at rung 1.
- A simple free win (Google profile check, review response) before the receptionist ask builds trust.
- **Plumbers first.** Master one trade fully before expanding to HVAC, electrical, roofing.
- AI stays behind the curtain. Sell the outcome ("stop missing calls"), never lead with "AI receptionist."
